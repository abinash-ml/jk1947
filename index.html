Project Motto & Vision

"Replacing pop-history myths with primary-source operational reality, rigorous tactical mapping, and multi-sided accountability."

Mainstream narratives frequently reduce the 1947–48 conflict to a narrow Valley-centric story, ignoring the catastrophic human and military struggle across the Jammu province, the defense of Poonch, the sieges of Skardu, and the logistical arteries from Pathankot.

Core Objectives

  • Primary Source Fidelity: Deconstruct war diaries, declassified despatches, MVC citations, and partisan memoirs (such as Akbar Khan's accounts).
  • Geospatial Rigor: Replace flat historical scans with modern terrain maps, NATO unit symbols, and elevation profiles.
  • Dual-Theater Balance: Anchor the narrative firmly in both the Jammu and Kashmir theaters with equal historical gravity.
  • Multi-Sided Record: Document Indian, Pakistani, British, and local state forces perspectives side by side.

A Note on the Creator & Project Journey

This digital archive is built entirely as an independent, leisure-time passion project. As a student navigating academic responsibilities and working to support my family, this research is a labor of love pursued during late nights and quiet weekends.

By releasing this work progressively through an independent workflow, the goal is to build a permanent, free, open historical resource that stands up to academic scrutiny. If this archive brings value to your understanding of military history, your support helps sustain the hosting, mapping tools, and deep archival research required to complete all 31 parts by November 1, 2029.
