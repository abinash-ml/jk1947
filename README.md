## 📌 Project Overview

**The Jammu & Kashmir War (1947–1948) Digital Archive** is an independent, 31-part interactive digital series and operational monograph. The project’s mission is to correct long-standing historiographical imbalances—specifically the frequent neglect of the **Jammu theater**—by synthesizing declassified primary sources, war diaries, unit citations, and modern geospatial mapping.

Developed as a personal research project in spare hours alongside academic and family commitments, this archive aims to serve as an authoritative, de-biased, open-access historical record for researchers, military historians, and the public.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Methodology

1. **Equal Operational Framing:** Provide equal weight to both major theaters—the **Jammu Province** (Poonch, Mirpur, Kotli, Jhanger, Naushera) and the **Kashmir & High Himalayan Sectors** (Jhelum Valley, Badgam, Zoji La, Leh, Skardu).
2. **Primary-Source Grounding:** Deconstruct operational logs, official battle citations (PVC/MVC), war diaries, declassified despatches, and multi-sided primary memoirs (including Indian, Pakistani, British, and J&K State Forces records).
3. **Geospatial Rigor:** Replace flat 1940s map scans with interactive terrain maps featuring dynamic timeline sliders, elevation profiles, and NATO military symbology (APP-6 / MIL-STD-2525).
4. **Multi-Perspectival Integrity:** Present side-by-side narrative matrices to document historical consensus, contested accounts, and unresolved questions transparently.

## 🗓️ 31-Part Master Series Structure

The archive is divided into six chronological phases leading to the 2029 launch:

* **Phase I: Prelude, Fractured Borders & The Initial Onslaught (Oct 1947)**
  * Parts 01–07 (Standstill State, Poonch Uprising, Op Gulmarg, Stand at Uri, Fall of Mirpur, Srinagar Airlift)
* **Phase II: The Siege of the Valley & Southern Desperation (Nov 1947)**
  * Parts 08–13 (Badgam, Shalateng, Jhanger/Rajouri Crises, Siege of Kotli, Poonch Lifeline)
* **Phase III: The Winter Campaigns & Diplomatic Entanglement (Dec 1947 – Feb 1948)**
  * Parts 14–18 (Naushera, Gilgit Mutiny/Skardu, UN Proceedings, Op Vijay I)
* **Phase IV: Spring Offensives & The High-Altitude Fronts (Mar 1948 – Jul 1948)**
  * Parts 19–24 (Op Rajouri, Siege of Skardu, Tithwal, Pakistani Regular Entry, Defense of Leh)
* **Phase V: The High-Altitude Armor Surge & The Southern Linkup (Aug 1948 – Dec 1948)**
  * Parts 25–29 (Op Bison/Zoji La Tanks, Kargil/Dras, Poonch Linkup, Fall of Skardu, Pir Kanthi)
* **Phase VI: The Ceasefire, Assessment & Historical Record (Jan 1949 & Beyond)**
  * Parts 30–31 (Midnight Ceasefire, Line of Control, Historiographical Legacy)

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Clean Vanilla HTML5 / CSS3 (Archival Light/Dark palette)
* **Mapping Engine:** Leaflet.js / Mapbox GL JS with georeferenced Survey of India 1940s map tiles (MapWarper)
* **Symbology:** Custom SVG vector icons rendered via NATO APP-6 standards
* **Data Layers:** GeoJSON for battle vectors and historical frontlines; JSON for client-side search (`Ctrl+K`)
* **Deployment:** Hosted statically under `abinash.net/jk1947/`
